# Experiment — Social Rehearsal Card

## Purpose

Test whether a disclosed imaginary social room can produce useful edits and risk awareness for a post, outfit, or creative artifact without pretending to be real social proof.

## Hypothesis

A small Room Contract plus synthetic role reactions will be more useful than a generic AI critique because it makes audience assumptions explicit and produces a debrief artifact.

## Setup

Input one artifact:

- short social post
- outfit image/description
- launch title
- thumbnail/copy pair
- difficult message draft

Run it through three rooms:

1. **Close Friends Room** — supportive but honest; looks for tone mismatch and overexplaining.
2. **Harsh Taste Room** — stylist/designer/critic roles; looks for genericness, wrong signal, weak taste.
3. **Confused Public Room** — casual strangers; looks for ambiguity, unintended read, missing context.

## Room Contract fields

- Room name
- Inhabitants / perspectives
- What they know
- What they do not know
- Tone
- Allowed reactions
- Forbidden reactions
- Memory policy
- Expiry
- Export format
- Warning label

## Output

A Social Rehearsal Card with:

- artifact tested
- room contracts
- strongest positive read
- strongest negative read
- predicted misreadings
- edit suggestions
- disagreement between rooms
- uncertainty
- publish/revise/ask-real-human/abandon recommendation
- explicit warning: synthetic reactions are not real consensus

## Success criteria

- The card identifies at least one useful misreading not obvious from generic critique.
- The room assumptions are visible enough that the user can reject them.
- The output helps decide whether to revise, publish, or test with a real person.
- No fake metrics, fake users, or claims of real-world consensus are produced.

## Failure criteria

- Reactions are generic praise/critique.
- The room feels like fake engagement rather than rehearsal.
- The output encourages reliance on synthetic approval.
- The room contract becomes too much setup friction for a casual user.

## Stopping condition

Stop after 10 artifacts across at least three artifact types. Continue only if the cards repeatedly produce specific revisions or decisions that a generic AI critique missed.

## Connected Vibes

- Social Apps Need Imaginary Rooms
- Empty Apps Can Launch Already Populated
- Wardrobes Need Simulation Memory
- Taste Engines Need Negative Space
- Personal AI Needs a Behavioral Canon
