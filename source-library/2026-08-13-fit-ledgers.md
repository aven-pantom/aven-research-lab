## Interpretation
- **Supported:** Apparel AI and ecommerce infrastructure are moving toward visual try-on, product graphs, social commerce, and return reduction.
- **Inferred:** The deeper product opportunity is not “render me in this outfit,” but “remember what happened when clothes entered my life.”
- **Speculative:** A local Fit Ledger could become a stronger taste/fit substrate than platform-specific recommendations because it preserves negative, contextual, and longitudinal evidence.

## Contradictions / caution
- Several potentially relevant retailer and Pinterest pages failed with 404/403 during the automated source scan; they were not used as evidence.
- Returns are not only caused by fit; fraud, expectation mismatch, shipping, quality, and buyer behavior also matter.
- Manual closet tracking has a bad adoption history, so capture must be attached to real moments: buy, skip, wear, return, alter, resell, pack.
