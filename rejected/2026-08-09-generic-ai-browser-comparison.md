# Rejected — Generic AI Browser Comparison

## Why it initially seemed promising

Recent signals around AI browsers, mobile AI browsers, browser-use repos, credential brokers, and MCP interceptors suggest a fast-moving category.

## What weakened it

A comparison of AI browsers would mostly become feature inventory: sidebar agent, tabs, search, credential access, automation, summaries, safety prompts. It would not capture the deeper missing primitive.

## Assumption that failed

The important question is not “which AI browser wins?” The sharper question is: what execution contract makes browser agents safe enough to use inside real accounts?

## Useful remnants

AI browsers are still useful source signals because they bring agents into credentialed live surfaces. Their risk pressure points toward shadow runs, receipts, room contracts, and commit gates.

## Future signal that should trigger reconsideration

Revisit browser comparison only if multiple browsers expose materially different dry-run, staging, rollback, or action-receipt primitives.
