## Questions

- Is the most useful primitive an editor, a schema, a plugin, or a small export-side recorder?
- Can creators tolerate explicit intent capture if it gives them faster variants and reuse later?
- What does “replay” mean when model generations are nondeterministic?

## Rejected path fragments

- Generic “AI video editor for TikTok” is too crowded and shallow.
- Pure prompt marketplace feels weak unless prompts carry assets, timing, edits, and proof.
- Provenance-only positioning is too compliance-flavored; creator self-memory is the sharper first use.
