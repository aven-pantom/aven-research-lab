## Selection logic

Chosen Sparks needed a visible behavior loop, not just a feature. Preference went to agent trust surfaces, creator workflows, short-form video behavior, local-first workspaces, fashion/taste, social coordination, and weird consumer utilities. Each Spark is small enough to prototype without depending on platform-scale network effects.

## Anti-slop filter

Rejected:

- generic AI wrappers around search/chat/video
- clones of dominant apps without a different loop
- monetization-only concepts
- ideas requiring unverified viral adoption before the first user gets value
- safety claims that would require real account, payment, aviation, or hardware operation to validate

## Strongest three to watch

1. **Shadow Run Previewer** — strongest trust primitive for browser/phone agents.
2. **Short-Drama Remix Desk** — rides visible short-drama and AI video/editor momentum without copying a feed.
3. **Agent Credential Risk Inbox** — turns credential brokers/interceptors into a consumer-readable habit loop.

## 1. Shadow Run Previewer

- **Trend signal:** HN scan surfaced Cloudflare Kitesurf, Buddy AI mobile browser, UnYOLO credential broker, and MCP interceptors; arXiv benchmarks keep emphasizing reproducible agent environments.
- **Existing proof:** Browser/computer-use agents are becoming a product category; WebArena, BrowserGym, WorkArena, OSWorld, and AndroidWorld show environment-based evaluation matters.
- **Gap:** Users see permission prompts and post-hoc logs, but rarely a predicted mutation before live execution.
- **Smallest prototype:** A local browser extension/mock app that turns one planned agent task into a Shadow Run Card: proposed actions, predicted changes, irreversible boundaries, approval gate, and receipt.
- **Why it could spread:** People share scary agent failures; a clean preview card is screenshot-friendly and gives builders a trust artifact.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Many websites cannot be mirrored; previews can become false reassurance.
- **Fit for Max:** Directly aligned with local-first agents, interface primitives, receipts, and Pantom/Orior agent operations.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**.

## 2. Agent Credential Risk Inbox

- **Trend signal:** HN scan surfaced UnYOLO as an agent credential broker and a real-time MCP interceptor that blocks `.env` reads and dangerous commands.
- **Existing proof:** Developers are already building policy engines, interceptors, and permission layers for agents touching GitHub, shell, env files, and tools.
- **Gap:** Current tools skew technical; the user needs a readable inbox of credential asks, denials, expirations, and observed use.
- **Smallest prototype:** A local dashboard that ingests mock MCP/tool permission events and groups them into “safe,” “risky,” “expired,” and “needs review.”
- **Why it could spread:** Every agent developer has credential anxiety; receipts create a visible artifact to share in teams.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Could become compliance UI nobody reads unless tied to real blocks and recovery.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with capability manifests, room contracts, behavioral canon, and agent ops.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**.

## 3. Short-Drama Remix Desk

- **Trend signal:** Apple Entertainment RSS top list included DramaWave, DramaBox, ReelShort, and StoryReel; Photo & Video top list included CapCut, Canva, Edits, and Cantina.
- **Existing proof:** Short-drama consumption and mobile video editing remain visibly high in app rankings.
- **Gap:** Creator tools optimize clips, not serialized micro-story testing: hook, reversal, cliffhanger, character card, scene economy, and variant reactions.
- **Smallest prototype:** A one-page storyboard/remix desk for 60-second drama beats with hook cards, cliffhanger variants, and export to CapCut/Edits.
- **Why it could spread:** Creators already share templates and “why this hook worked” breakdowns; remixable beat cards are social objects.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Short-drama economics may be content-farm driven; independent creators may not want structured writing tools.
- **Fit for Max:** Bridges creative tools, media funnels, and taste-led product worlds.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**.

## 4. Local-First Writing Room With Agent Receipts

- **Trend signal:** HN scan surfaced Writer.computer and local-first desktop interest; GitHub scan surfaced local-first AI desktop workspaces.
- **Existing proof:** Markdown/local-first apps and agent workspaces continue appearing in developer channels.
- **Gap:** Writing tools rarely show which suggestions came from which source, memory, or agent action.
- **Smallest prototype:** A calm Markdown editor where every agent edit creates a sidecar receipt: source used, text changed, intent, confidence, undo patch.
- **Why it could spread:** Writers and builders can share before/after receipts as proof of process, not just final text.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Writers may prefer invisible assistance; receipt overhead could break flow.
- **Fit for Max:** High fit with research portal, provenance, local-first workshops, and calm editorial UI.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**.

## 5. MCP Video Edit Button

- **Trend signal:** HN scan surfaced Shorz adding an MCP server so Claude can edit videos and a Rust video editor with AI features.
- **Existing proof:** AI-assisted video editing is moving from generation into tool-control and timeline operation.
- **Gap:** Agent video edits need small, inspectable commands and reaction evidence, not opaque “make it better” prompts.
- **Smallest prototype:** A mock MCP video tool with three verbs: cut silence, add caption beat, create variant; each returns an Intent/Reaction Card.
- **Why it could spread:** Demoable as “Claude edited my timeline, but here is the receipt.”
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Serious editors may need deep NLE integration before caring.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with AI video intent timelines and creative provenance.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**.

## 7. Outfit Boundary Rejection Camera

- **Trend signal:** Apple Lifestyle RSS showed Pinterest high; Photo & Video showed camera/editor apps high; prior lab work keeps finding negative taste events as underused signals.
- **Existing proof:** People already save fashion references and use visual apps for taste collection.
- **Gap:** Most tools capture likes and outfits worn; they miss “almost wore,” “looked wrong,” “too polished,” “not me,” and context failures.
- **Smallest prototype:** A camera log for rejected outfits with one-tap reason chips and a weekly boundary map.
- **Why it could spread:** Rejection grids are intimate, visual, and relatable; they make taste feel sharper than recommendations.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Users may not want to document failed looks unless the payoff is immediate.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fashion/taste-engine fit and connects to negative taste cards.
- **Evidence label:** **Inferred**.

## 8. Agent Task Board for Parallel Coders

- **Trend signal:** GitHub scan surfaced `compozy/compozy` and `bernatch22/taskops`; HN scan surfaced coding harnesses and structured file-edit tools.
- **Existing proof:** Multi-agent coding workflows are becoming operational rather than speculative.
- **Gap:** Teams need a small shared board that prevents agents from grabbing the same work, exposes branch/commit ownership, and summarizes risk.
- **Smallest prototype:** A Markdown/JSON task board with atomic claims, branch names, last action, touched files, and merge readiness.
- **Why it could spread:** Coding-agent teams create coordination failures immediately; a lightweight board is easy to adopt.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Existing coding platforms may absorb the feature quickly.
- **Fit for Max:** Fits Hermes/Aven operations and agent-maintained tools.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**.

## 9. Drone Route Weather Receipt Mock

- **Trend signal:** Existing lab direction on Air Street Condition Cards; no new aviation product scan dominated tonight, but route-local evidence remains a high-alignment frontier.
- **Existing proof:** Prior Vibe work established the gap between airport forecasts and low-altitude drone route conditions; aviation interfaces require conservative evidence.
- **Gap:** Drone-route planning tools can become misleading if they show generic green routes without freshness/source uncertainty.
- **Smallest prototype:** A simulator-only route card with fictional wind, gust, obstruction, source freshness, uncertainty, and conservative no-go language.
- **Why it could spread:** Drone builders and autonomy designers need legible evidence cards before operational claims.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Without real data integration, it remains a design artifact.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong Notrex/N1 Scout interface fit, if kept non-operational and simulator-only.
- **Evidence label:** **Inferred**.

## 10. Personal AI Home Dock

- **Trend signal:** Apple Productivity RSS top list is dominated by AI assistants; GitHub scan surfaced local-first personal AI assistants and desktop workspaces.
- **Existing proof:** Users have multiple AI assistants, but little local doctrine about which one handles what.
- **Gap:** Personal AI is spread across apps; users need a local dock that maps domains, boundaries, memory policies, and preferred agents.
- **Smallest prototype:** A desktop/local web “AI home dock” with cards for writing, code, research, errands, fashion, and admin; each card defines allowed agents, memories, and receipts.
- **Why it could spread:** People already compare ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini/Grok; a personal routing surface makes that behavior durable.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Assistant platforms may prevent deep integration; manual setup may be too much.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with behavioral canon, local-first agents, and Aven identity/project memory.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**.
