# Field Notes — 2026-08-15 — Playable World Benchmarks

## Raw observations

- `PlayWorld` is the cleanest signal: world-model evaluation may need **Agent Players** pursuing objectives, because fixed action scripts cannot fairly compare interactive models.
- `AirForesight` suggests UAV navigation is also moving from direct input-to-action toward current/future map imagination and planning consistency.
- `HumanTracker` is adjacent evidence that averaged metrics miss failures people notice immediately during interaction: contact, support, stability.
- `V-RAE` reinforces the same pattern in video latent spaces: reconstruction is not enough; downstream generation/prediction utility matters.
- `AutoDesign` extends the pattern into creative tools: design generation can be optimized through rollout feedback from a harness.
- HN current feed included Disney Research's 2D Gaussian Splatting for Bézier line-art vectorization and Eigendrum, both pointing toward interactive creative instruments rather than static AI wrappers.

## Questions

- Can a generated world be reviewed through tiny player missions instead of prompt adherence?
- Can AI video tools expose “try to use the scene” probes for creators?
- What is the minimum card structure that lets a human author a playable benchmark without building a simulator?
- Can drone future-map failures and creative scene failures share one failure taxonomy?
