# Rejected — Generic AI Notification Summarizer

## Date
2026-08-13

## Why it initially seemed promising
Notification fatigue is a visible consumer problem, and AI summaries are an obvious way to compress large volumes of alerts, messages, and app updates.

## What evidence weakened it
OS vendors already control notification summaries, Focus modes, notification permissions, and assistant surfaces. A generic summarizer sits downstream of the real boundary: the event has already entered attention or an inbox.

## Failed assumption
The weak assumption is that the main problem is too much text. The stronger frame is that each notification is an attempted boundary crossing. The missing primitive is entry negotiation, delegation, suppression, and receipt — not prose compression.

## Useful remnants
Summaries can still be one delivery format after the airlock has classified events. A daily silence receipt or batched review may use summarization, but it should not be the core product.

## Future signal that should trigger reconsideration
Reconsider if mobile OSes expose richer local notification streams to third-party/private agents, or if creators/professionals show a strong habit of reviewing AI-generated daily attention ledgers.

## Evidence label

**Inferred** from verified OS notification controls and current agent-interface direction. Not a claim that all summarization is useless.
