# Trend Sparks — 2026-08-10

Exactly 10 current, evidence-backed app opportunities. These are Trend Sparks, not generic ideas.

## 1. Reference Contract Board

- **Trend signal:** Reference-driven AI creative tools are becoming normal: Runway Gen-4 emphasizes consistent characters/objects/locations from references; Figma Make and Krea embed generation into creative workspaces.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** Runway, Figma Make, and Krea pages were reachable during this run and describe AI creative generation/editing surfaces.
- **Gap:** References are still loose inspiration, not typed constraints with protected properties and allowed mutations.
- **Smallest prototype:** Static local board where 12 references become cards with role, protected properties, allowed mutations, forbidden mutations, and output check.
- **Why it could spread:** Creators can share a contract board with collaborators or clients instead of rewriting taste in comments.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Too much structure may slow exploration; model compliance may be unreliable.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with creative tools, Pantom/Orior taste, provenance, and local-first artifacts.
- **Evidence label:** Supported.

## 2. Variant Violation Inbox

- **Trend signal:** AI video/image tools make rapid variants easy, but review still happens by subjective eye.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** Runway Gen-4 and Krea market generation/editing capabilities; C2PA shows provenance assertions are standardizable after creation.
- **Gap:** Creators need a lightweight inbox of “what changed that should not have changed.”
- **Smallest prototype:** Upload two outputs plus a Reference Contract Card; manually or semi-automatically list invariant violations and suggested repairs.
- **Why it could spread:** It turns vague client feedback into a shareable receipt.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Violation detection may be too subjective for mood/style references.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with Reaction Cards, creative provenance, and high-taste review workflows.
- **Evidence label:** Inferred.

## 3. Tap-to-Teach Reference Roles

- **Trend signal:** Creators collect references constantly, but AI tools rarely ask what each reference is for.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** Pinterest exists as a mainstream visual collection surface; Figma Make/Krea provide AI creative workspaces.
- **Gap:** A saved image can mean palette, composition, typography, identity, texture, lighting, or “avoid this.”
- **Smallest prototype:** Browser/share-sheet tool that lets a creator save an image and tap one role label plus one protected property.
- **Why it could spread:** Low-friction saves create a portable taste/workflow asset over time.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Users may not consistently label references unless immediate generation improves.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with taste engines and local personal software.
- **Evidence label:** Supported.

## 4. Short-Form Edit Receipt

- **Trend signal:** Mobile video editors remain central to creator workflows.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** CapCut and Instagram Edits App Store listings were reachable during this run.
- **Gap:** Short-form edits are easy to export but hard to reuse as structured creative memory.
- **Smallest prototype:** Import a 15-second edit plan and export a card listing clips, text beats, transitions, references, rejected variants, and next remix prompt.
- **Why it could spread:** Creators can share the recipe behind a style without handing over raw projects.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Existing editor project files may already satisfy power users.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with creator funnels and video intent timelines.
- **Evidence label:** Supported.

## 5. Agent PR Risk Desk

- **Trend signal:** Cloud/browser coding agents are moving from chat toward delegated work.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** Anthropic’s Claude Code on the web page says users can delegate coding tasks from the browser to managed cloud infrastructure.
- **Gap:** Users need a small review desk that surfaces branch scope, files touched, commands run, risks, and rollback path before merge.
- **Smallest prototype:** GitHub PR sidebar generator that reads a diff and produces a risk card plus “safe to squash?” checklist.
- **Why it could spread:** Every agent-made PR creates anxiety; teams can pass around a readable risk card.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** GitHub checks and reviews may already cover enough for many teams.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with Hermes/Aven workflows, agent receipts, and autonomous repo operation.
- **Evidence label:** Supported.

## 6. MCP Tool Shelf for Normal People

- **Trend signal:** Agent tools are becoming installable capabilities.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** MCP documentation was reachable and frames the Model Context Protocol as a standard context/tooling layer.
- **Gap:** Non-developers need to know what a tool can touch, whether it is reversible, and when to revoke it.
- **Smallest prototype:** Local shelf of five MCP tools with permission envelope, sample trace, failure modes, and revoke instructions.
- **Why it could spread:** People share “safe tool packs” if trust and scope are legible.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** MCP adoption may stay developer-centric or fragment by host.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with tool manifests, local-first agents, and Pantom infrastructure.
- **Evidence label:** Supported.

## 7. Drone Intent Receipt Viewer

- **Trend signal:** Civil drone legibility is increasingly regulatory and interface-driven.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** FAA Remote ID page was reachable and describes Remote ID as active.
- **Gap:** Remote ID identifies drones, but local observers and operators still lack human-readable intent and freshness cues.
- **Smallest prototype:** Simulator-only viewer that converts a fictional route into expiring intent cards: purpose, altitude band, route segment, privacy level, abort condition.
- **Why it could spread:** Trust improves when autonomous movement has manners, not only identifiers.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Regulatory and privacy constraints may make public intent too sensitive.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with N1 Scout/Notrex-adjacent civil aviation interfaces.
- **Evidence label:** Inferred.

## 8. Spatial Reference Pinboard

- **Trend signal:** Spatial computing makes reference placement physical rather than tab-based.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** Apple Vision Pro page says digital content blends with physical space.
- **Gap:** Reference boards in spatial environments need persistence, roles, and scale/lighting context, not floating screenshots.
- **Smallest prototype:** Desktop/web mock where references are pinned to zones: wall, desk, body, object, timeline, with role labels.
- **Why it could spread:** Designers and creators can walk around a project’s visual logic instead of scrolling a folder.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Spatial workflows may remain niche and hardware-constrained.
- **Fit for Max:** Good fit with interface primitives and creative tool worlds.
- **Evidence label:** Inferred.

## 9. Local Brand Canon Extractor

- **Trend signal:** AI design tools generate fast, but brand consistency is still manually policed.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** Figma Make page describes AI-powered design creation; C2PA shows structured assertions can attach to media.
- **Gap:** Small brands need a local, inspectable canon extracted from approved/rejected work rather than a PDF brand book.
- **Smallest prototype:** Drop 20 approved assets and 10 rejected assets into a local folder; produce a Brand Canon Card with rules, examples, negative examples, and uncertainty.
- **Why it could spread:** Agencies and creators can hand agents a living taste contract.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Extraction may flatten taste into generic rules unless examples stay visible.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with Orior/Pantom and behavioral canon research.
- **Evidence label:** Inferred.

## 10. Creator Rights Prep Sheet

- **Trend signal:** Provenance standards and AI creative workflows are converging, but creators still prepare rights context manually.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** C2PA specification exists for content provenance; AI creative tools such as Runway/Krea/Figma Make are active surfaces.
- **Gap:** Before publishing, creators need a simple sheet listing source assets, rights assumptions, reference roles, generated elements, and uncertainty.
- **Smallest prototype:** Markdown/JSON prep sheet generated from a project folder and exported beside final media.
- **Why it could spread:** It is useful even before formal standards adoption because it reduces client/platform confusion.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Legal usefulness may be limited without platform/tool integration.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with provenance sandboxes and creator infrastructure.
- **Evidence label:** Supported.
