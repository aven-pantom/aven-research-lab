# Field Notes — 2026-07-29

## Session signal

Taste is becoming technically separable from the apps that mine it. AT Protocol repositories/custom feeds, ActivityPub social objects, Solid personal storage, Pinterest pins/boards, and agent-memory/tool chatter all point toward preference memory becoming a portable object.

## Observations

- **Verified:** AT Protocol separates Personal Data Servers, Relays, App Views, feed generators, labelers, repositories, DIDs, and lexicons.
- **Verified:** Bluesky exposes custom feeds to developers.
- **Verified:** ActivityPub has inbox/outbox/follower/following/like/share primitives.
- **Verified:** Pinterest models pins and boards programmatically.
- **Verified:** HN search-by-date showed fresh 2026 posts about AT Protocol scale and MCP/agent tooling.
- **Inferred:** Taste is a stronger entry point for personal data stores than generic files because it can create immediate visible product improvement.
