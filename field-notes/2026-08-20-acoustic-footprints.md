# Field Notes — 2026-08-20 — Acoustic Footprints

## Starting signal

NASA's `Noise Exposure Maps of Urban Air Mobility` uses simulated UAM routes, vertiports, local land use, noise-sensitive facilities, and AIRNOISEUAM predictions to build UAM noise exposure maps. That feels like an early version of a missing civil-autonomy object: not a vehicle spec, but a route-local acoustic evidence card.

## Observations

- **Verified:** NASA has treated UAM noise as geographic exposure, not just aircraft performance.
- **Verified:** FAA has a public aviation-noise policy/guidance surface, which means drone acoustic work sits in a public legitimacy domain.
- **Supported:** Low-altitude drone acceptance may fail through repetition, tone, time-of-day, surprise, and perceived disrespect even if raw sound levels look technically acceptable.
- **Inferred:** A route can be legal, connected, and weather-clear while still being locally wrong because its acoustic footprint crosses schools, clinics, quiet parks, residential sleep windows, or cultural expectations.

## Why it feels important

The last drone Vibes mapped air, radio, intent, modules, and weather residuals. Noise is the missing public-facing condition. Radio weather is mostly invisible to neighbors; acoustic weather is the part everyone experiences.

## Fast branches followed

- NASA NTRS UAM noise exposure maps.
- NASA AAM environmental impact presentation.
- FAA aviation-noise guidance hub.
- Prior lab evidence-card grammar: Weather Autopsy Cards, Radio Weather Cards, Airspace Manners.

## Rejected path during scan

Generic drone-noise complaint app. It starts from grievance capture rather than from route evidence, residuals, and design constraints. Useful as a reporting channel later, but not the primitive.

## Questions

- Can an Acoustic Footprint Card remain useful without implying legal clearance or community consent?
- What should be represented: DNL, maximum event level, tonal quality, repetition count, time window, background contrast, sensitive-place flags, or human reports?
- Should acoustic cards be pre-flight forecasts, post-flight autopsies, public notices, or all three with different language?
- Can a simulator-only deck expose false-confidence language before any operational data is touched?

## Secondary signals

- Trend Sparks keep pointing toward consumer trust receipts: browser-agent tickets, creator proof cards, local utilities, and drone public explainer cards.
- The stronger convergence is not “more dashboards.” It is small evidence objects that travel with actions.
