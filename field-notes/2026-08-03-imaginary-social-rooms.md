# Field Notes — 2026-08-03 — Imaginary Social Rooms

## Starting signal

Apple iTunes Search surfaced visible traction around AI-only social apps: `status - sims but social media`, `Aspect: AI-Only Social Media`, `Fomo - AI Social Network`, and `Charm: AI Only Social Media`.

## Observations

- **Verified:** `status - sims but social media` had 188,980 ratings on 2026-08-03. Store copy centers persona creation, fandoms, characters as followers, posts, fame, and cancellation.
- **Verified:** `Aspect: AI-Only Social Media` had 8,769 ratings. Store copy explicitly says the user is the only human and can post to AI characters, comment on AI feeds, DM AI characters, and create characters.
- **Verified:** `Fomo - AI Social Network` had 2,409 ratings. Store copy says there are no real people and every account, comment, and message is AI-powered.
- **Verified:** `Charm: AI Only Social Media` had 75 ratings. Store copy says it looks like social media and the user is the only human.
- **Verified:** arXiv search surfaced `Mental World Modeling` (2026-07-29), which argues that behavior modeling needs hidden mental state and social permissibility, not only physical state.
- **Verified:** arXiv search surfaced `Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior`, which explicitly includes rehearsal spaces for interpersonal communication among possible applications.
- **Verified:** GitHub API showed active open-source interest around social/generative-agent infrastructure: `generative_agents`, `ai-town`, `camel`, and `concordia`.

## Contradictions

- The consumer language sells pressure-free synthetic attention, while the deeper useful primitive is rehearsal and debrief.
- Rating counts show attention, not whether the behavior is healthy, retained, or worth building around.
- A social simulator can improve expression or make the user dependent on agreement from non-people.
