# Rejected — Generic Offline Chatbot

## Why it initially seemed promising

Local models, Apple Foundation Models, tiny agentic models, and WebNN all make offline AI feel more feasible. A simple offline personal chatbot looks like the obvious consumer app.

## What weakened it

- **Supported:** Chat is not the hard part of always-on local agency. Presence, permissions, wake conditions, memory retention, battery, and receipts are harder.
- **Verified:** `Jagarin` frames persistent mobile agents as constrained by battery and platform sandboxing.
- **Verified:** `Tiny Enough to Break In` frames small local agentic models as a security risk.
- **Inferred:** A generic offline chatbot would not test the resident-control problem.

## Failed assumption

The assumption was that local inference alone is the differentiator. The stronger differentiator is scoped resident duty.

## Useful remnants

- Offline chat may be one escalation target.
- Local model packaging and benchmarking still matter.
- A chatbot can explain receipts, but should not be the core primitive.

## Future signal that should trigger reconsideration

Reconsider if a local chatbot develops persistent app/folder/device duties with explicit receipts, safe revocation, and cross-device continuity rather than only offline question answering.
