# Rejected — Generic Real-Time AI Assistant

## Why it initially seemed promising

OmniAssistBench and live-agent infrastructure make real-time assistants feel timely. The obvious product framing is a general always-on AI helper for video, screen, and voice.

## What weakened it

The generic assistant framing collapses the useful signal into an app category. It hides the sharper problem: intervention timing. A model can be live, multimodal, and context-rich while still speaking at the wrong moment.

## Failed assumption

“Real-time + multimodal + agentic” is not the primitive. The primitive is phase-aware help: when to observe, wait, interrupt, prevent, guide, or debrief.

## Could become viable later

A general assistant may become viable if built on explicit Moment Sense, Attention Airlocks, Resident Duty Cards, Permission Receipts, and local custody.

## Useful remnants

- Live voice/video agent frameworks are useful infrastructure.
- Local continuous computer history can supply context.
- Assistant benchmarks can become timing/intervention benchmarks.

## Future reconsideration trigger

Reconsider if a product or paper shows users actively teaching interruption timing, or if a local model can reliably classify task phase from low-privacy cues.
