# Field Notes — 2026-08-13 — Attention Airlocks

## Signal

Notification systems are already permissioned and context-aware at the OS level, while personal agents are moving toward resident, always-on duty layers. The strange gap is that agent attention requests still tend to collapse into either chat messages, push notifications, hidden automation, or summaries.

## Notes

- Apple Focus and Android notification permission both imply attention is a boundary, not just a UI surface.
- Android conversation notifications are an early category split: people/conversations are treated differently from generic app events.
- App Intents points toward app actions/content being exposed to system layers; future attention handling could route some events into local action rather than human interruption.
- The E-MIIM arXiv paper is older and telephony-focused, but useful because it names “intelligent interruption management” as a modeling domain.
- The opportunity is not “AI notification summarizer.” Summaries still assume the event entered the mental room.
- A better primitive is an entry declaration: why now, what expires, what happens if ignored, who can handle it, what receipt remains.

## Rejected path during search

- Generic notification fatigue trend search was noisy and returned irrelevant “fatigue” materials from engineering/materials. Better queries use interruptibility, intelligent interruption management, and OS notification APIs.

## Question becoming stronger

Can a personal agent earn trust by proving not only what it did, but what it noticed and chose not to interrupt?
