# Unexpected Connection — Fit Ledgers and Aircraft Maintenance Logbooks

## Connection
Fit Ledgers and aircraft maintenance logbooks solve the same hidden problem: they preserve anomaly memory across time so the next decision is not made from the clean surface alone.

## Shared structure
- A visible object appears acceptable: an aircraft is airworthy-looking; an outfit looks good in a try-on render.
- Small anomalies accumulate: vibration, recurring inspection notes, component replacement history; pinching, ride-up, never-worn events, repeated returns.
- Context matters: weather, hours, load, maintenance action; climate, posture, event, layering, fabric behavior.
- The useful artifact is a typed ledger of conditions, interventions, outcomes, and confidence.

## Why it matters
Fashion software over-trusts the current visual surface in the same way unsafe maintenance cultures can over-trust a clean inspection moment. The durable intelligence is in the record of friction: what failed before, under which condition, and what changed after intervention.

## Transferable mechanism
Maintenance logbooks separate observation, action, part/context, and outcome. A Fit Ledger Card can borrow that grammar:

- observation: what felt or looked wrong
- condition: where/when/body/weather/movement
- intervention: return, tailor, resell, style differently, ignore
- outcome: worn more, still avoided, solved, failed
- confidence: one-off anomaly or recurring pattern

## Boundary
This is a product/interface analogy, not operational aviation guidance. The value is the ledger structure, not any transfer of safety procedures into consumer fashion.
