Sources checked: AT Protocol overview/repository docs, Bluesky custom feed docs, ActivityPub W3C Recommendation, Solid Protocol, Pinterest API v5 docs, FAA Remote ID page, GitHub pages for Model Context Protocol and browser-use, plus Hacker News Algolia search-by-date for `atproto` and `MCP agent` on 2026-07-29. ## Selection logic

These 10 made the cut because each has an existing behavior or infrastructure proof and a non-generic product loop. Excluded: generic AI chat wrappers, broad social-network clones, vague shopping assistants, and pure monetization tactics without a repeated user behavior.

## 10 Trend Sparks

### 1. Taste Passport

- **Trend signal:** Social protocols and personal-data-store patterns are making identity/data portable while fashion/design apps still trap saves, boards, screenshots, and rejects.
- **Existing proof:** AT Protocol documents PDS/repositories/app views/feed generators; Solid defines personal data resources; Pinterest exposes Pins and Boards APIs.
- **Gap:** No consumer-feeling app lets taste move across wardrobe, moodboard, shopping, and creator discovery.
- **Smallest prototype:** Local-first `taste-cards.jsonl` with 50 manual save/wear/reject events, a visual taste diff, and export/import.
- **Why it could spread:** Users can share tasteful graph slices: `my summer reference stack`, `my jacket rules`, `things I always reject`.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Users may not care about portability unless the immediate interface is beautiful and useful.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fashion/taste-engine and local-first overlap; could connect to wardrobes, creative references, and brand worlds.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported** for infrastructure; **Speculative** for consumer demand.

### 2. Custom Feed Foundry for Small Communities

- **Trend signal:** Bluesky custom feeds and AT Protocol app-view/feed separation make ranking feel like a buildable community object.
- **Existing proof:** Bluesky developer docs expose custom-feed starter templates; AT Protocol overview names feed generators as supporting services.
- **Gap:** Non-technical communities cannot author feed rules, taste labels, source lists, and moderation boundaries in a calm visual tool.
- **Smallest prototype:** A hosted/custom-feed builder with three rule types: include accounts, boost labels/keywords, downrank sources; export feed metadata and receipts.
- **Why it could spread:** Communities share feeds as cultural objects, not just follow lists.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Platform/feed API constraints may limit expressiveness; communities may prefer default algorithms.
- **Fit for Max:** Social product plus interface primitive; good fit for Pantom/Orior-style community funnels.
- **Evidence label:** **Verified** for custom-feed surface; **Inferred** for builder gap.

### 3. Negative Taste Logger

- **Trend signal:** Recommendation systems over-index on positive saves/likes while users constantly reject styles, products, sounds, and creators silently.
- **Existing proof:** Pinterest boards/pins and social likes/shares are structured positive signals; scanned specs expose few rich negative preference objects.
- **Gap:** Personalization tools rarely remember `not this shape`, `not this fabric`, `not this editing style`, or `not this creator vibe` across apps.
- **Smallest prototype:** One-tap reject diary for fashion/design/media with reason chips, weekly pattern summary, and agent-readable export.
- **Why it could spread:** `Things I refuse to wear/use/watch` is socially expressive and useful for shopping/styling agents.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Could feel negative or tedious unless the interface is playful and fast.
- **Fit for Max:** Taste-engine moat; turns a subtle design judgment into data without generic AI wrapper behavior.
- **Evidence label:** **Inferred** from source gaps and existing positive-signal structures.

### 4. Agent Context Wallet

- **Trend signal:** HN search-by-date surfaced active MCP/agent tooling discussions; agents need bounded context across tools.
- **Existing proof:** Model Context Protocol has a public GitHub organization; HN results included MCP/agent items dated 2026-07-28/29.
- **Gap:** Users lack a simple way to lend an agent just the right slice of personal context, then revoke it.
- **Smallest prototype:** Local wallet of context cards with scopes, expiry, and receipts: `style`, `calendar norms`, `brand voice`, `coding prefs`.
- **Why it could spread:** Every agent task starts with `use this card`, making trust and personalization visible.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Protocol churn could make the integration layer unstable.
- **Fit for Max:** Direct bridge between local-first agents, Aven/Hermes workflows, and personal software.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported** for agent-tool momentum; **Inferred** for consumer UX gap.

### 5. Browser Agent Street-Test Lab

- **Trend signal:** Browser automation for agents is becoming accessible, but web behavior still lacks visible compliance tests.
- **Existing proof:** browser-use GitHub page describes making websites accessible for AI agents and automating online tasks.
- **Gap:** Product teams cannot easily see how an agent reads, clicks, gets stuck, or violates intended paths on their site.
- **Smallest prototype:** Drop-in site test that runs three disclosed agent personas and outputs a replayable friction/compliance report.
- **Why it could spread:** Founders/designers share `agent-readiness scores` as launch hygiene.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Sites may not prioritize agent readiness until agent traffic is material.
- **Fit for Max:** Extends recent agent street-sign/flight-recorder Vibes into a practical audit product.
- **Evidence label:** **Verified** for browser-use existence; **Inferred** for readiness demand.

### 6. Drone Intent Overlay

- **Trend signal:** Civil drone integration needs legible identification and intent, not only raw aircraft presence.
- **Existing proof:** FAA Remote ID page describes remote identification requirements for drones.
- **Gap:** Remote ID does not give nearby humans a simple, calming explanation of what a drone is doing, for how long, and under what boundary.
- **Smallest prototype:** Simulator/mobile overlay that converts compliant public drone metadata plus operator-declared intent into plain-language cards.
- **Why it could spread:** Neighborhood, event, campus, and public-safety contexts need trust surfaces before dense drone operations.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Regulatory and data-access constraints may block real-world utility.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong drones/aviation interface lane; non-hardware, safety-forward entry.
- **Evidence label:** **Verified** for Remote ID; **Inferred** for intent-card gap.

### 7. Creator Source Board With Receipts

- **Trend signal:** Creators build work from scattered references, but public posts often hide the source trail and taste decisions.
- **Existing proof:** Pinterest pins/boards prove saved references are structured behavior; ActivityPub and ATProto show posts/social objects can be protocol-level artifacts.
- **Gap:** There is no tasteful creator-facing board that turns references into public, attributable process cards without becoming a Notion clone.
- **Smallest prototype:** Markdown/visual board: add sources, annotate why they matter, export a share card and source receipt.
- **Why it could spread:** Process receipts create credibility, taste signaling, and remixable education.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Creators may protect references instead of sharing them.
- **Fit for Max:** Fits content funnels, Orior dossiers, creative tools, and research-lab publishing.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported** for reference-board behavior; **Speculative** for sharing appetite.

### 8. Local-First Feed Diary

- **Trend signal:** Federated feeds and custom feeds expose ranking as configurable, while users still cannot inspect why their own attention changed.
- **Existing proof:** AT Protocol custom feed and repository primitives; ActivityPub collections/likes/shares.
- **Gap:** Users lack a private diary of feeds they saw, saved, muted, and regretted across social apps.
- **Smallest prototype:** Browser/mobile capture of daily feed samples with local labels: energizing, draining, useful, copied, ignored.
- **Why it could spread:** `My week in algorithm weather` is a shareable but privacy-preserving artifact.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Capture permissions and platform ToS may be painful.
- **Fit for Max:** Weird consumer utility with strong local-first/social product character.
- **Evidence label:** **Inferred** from protocol/feed movement and attention-tracking gap.

### 9. Wardrobe Return Radar

- **Trend signal:** Style engines need to know not only what was bought/worn but what was returned, unused, or repeatedly skipped.
- **Existing proof:** Pinterest pins/boards and taste artifacts exist; portable Taste Card concept can represent bought/returned/skipped events.
- **Gap:** Fashion apps rarely treat returns and unworn items as high-value negative training data.
- **Smallest prototype:** Manual closet log: item, reason returned/skipped, occasion mismatch, material/fit note, monthly `do not buy again` rules.
- **Why it could spread:** It saves money and produces brutally useful personal style rules.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Manual logging may feel like chores unless tied to purchases/photos.
- **Fit for Max:** Direct extension of wardrobe simulation memory with a sharper behavior loop.
- **Evidence label:** **Inferred**; strongest as prototype candidate, not validated trend.

### 10. Protocol Launch Cards

- **Trend signal:** New protocols, agent tools, and federated systems are appearing faster than normal users can understand why they matter.
- **Existing proof:** HN 2026-07-28/29 results showed AT Protocol and MCP/agent tooling chatter in the same scan window.
- **Gap:** Protocol launches are explained to developers, not to product/design people deciding whether a new primitive changes what can be built.
- **Smallest prototype:** One-page card generator for protocols: new primitive, old constraint removed, first app loop, risk, migration path, source receipts.
- **Why it could spread:** Designers/founders share clear `what this unlocks` cards instead of raw spec links.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Could degrade into newsletter slop if not source-first.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong Orior/Pantom/content funnel fit and useful for Aven research synthesis.
- **Evidence label:** **Verified** for current protocol chatter; **Inferred** for card demand.

## Anti-slop filter

Rejected:

- `AI fashion stylist chatbot`: too generic without explicit taste memory.
- `Federated TikTok clone`: copies a dominant app without a new loop.
- `MCP marketplace`: monetization-first and crowded unless tied to trust/context cards.
- `Drone social network`: weak behavior loop and dual-use complexity.
- `Personal data pod for everything`: too abstract; taste-specific loop is sharper.

## Strongest three to watch

1. Taste Passport
2. Agent Context Wallet
3. Browser Agent Street-Test Lab
