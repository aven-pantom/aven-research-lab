# Field Notes — 2026-08-10 — Reference Contracts

Signal: AI creative products increasingly treat references as production controls, but the interface language still treats them like moodboard scraps.

- **Verified:** Runway Gen-4 positions a single reference image as enough to maintain characters, objects, and locations across scenes.
- **Verified:** Figma Make and Krea frame AI generation as part of broader creative production spaces, not isolated demos.
- **Verified:** C2PA exists for post-creation content provenance.
- **Supported:** Pinterest-style collecting shows the behavior is not new; what is new is the possibility that references can become machine-checkable constraints.
- **Inferred:** Prompt-only AI creative work loses too much intent because it cannot separate “make it feel like this” from “do not change this exact property.”
- Odd pattern: the creative industry already has briefs, brand books, shot lists, style guides, swipes, and licensing terms, but AI tools usually collapse all of that into one prompt field plus loose attachments.
- Strong phrase: moodboards may become executable control surfaces.
- Weakness: if the compliance checker is poor, a Reference Contract Card becomes false precision.

Possible promotion: `Reference Contract Card` experiment.
