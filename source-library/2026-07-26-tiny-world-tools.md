### Impeccable

- URL: https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable
- Raw README: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/README.md
- Evidence label: **Verified**
- Notes:
  - README says: “Design guidance for AI coding agents. 1 skill, 23 commands, live browser iteration, and 60 deterministic detector rules for AI-generated frontend design.”
  - It targets repeated generic AI design tells: same SaaS templates, Inter everywhere, purple/blue gradients, nested cards, weak hierarchy.
  - It creates `PRODUCT.md` and offers `DESIGN.md` so later commands know audience, brand/product lane, voice, anti-references, colors, type, and components.

## Challenge / contradiction scan

- HyperCard-style environments have appeared repeatedly and mostly stayed niche.
- Broad cloud documents and general AI assistants have stronger distribution.
- Constraints can become aesthetic nostalgia if they do not compress real work.
- The open question is whether “tiny worlds” become shareable durable artifacts or merely charming demos.

## Rejected paths

- Treating Decker as only a retro-computing note. Too shallow; the stronger signal is constrained agency under AI delegation.
- Treating Impeccable as only frontend linting. The deeper primitive is making taste operational for agents.
- Building a “new HyperCard” claim. Too generic and historically loaded. The better frame is portable worldlets with agent-readable rules.
