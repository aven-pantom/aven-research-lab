## 1. Air Street Condition Cards

- **Trend signal:** Civil low-altitude autonomy needs route-local weather evidence, not only broad forecasts.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** FAA UTM page exists for low-altitude UAS traffic-management context; NOAA HRRR page warns its public research data should not be used for safety-of-life decisions; UCAR/NCAR story title directly links urban weather forecasting to drones and air taxis; OpenAlex/Crossref surfaced `An Initial Assessment of the Potential Weather Barriers of Urban Air Mobility`.
- **Gap:** Forecasts and maps do not explain whether a specific route segment is inside a bounded operational envelope.
- **Smallest prototype:** Simulator-only Markdown/JSON route card for one fake 2 km drone route with wind, gust, visibility, freshness, source, and go/caution/unknown status.
- **Why it could spread:** Drone educators, operators, and interface designers need safer ways to discuss weather risk without pretending clearance.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Real aviation weather support is safety-critical and regulated; a standalone app could be irresponsible outside simulation/planning UX.
- **Fit for Max:** Very high; connects drones, aviation interfaces, N1 Scout-style systems, and evidence-card design.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**

## 2. Event Drop Companion

- **Trend signal:** Event-specific social apps are visible in mainstream charts.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** Apple U.S. top-free feed ranked `TikTok Pro - Events` at #2 on 2026-08-02 scan.
- **Gap:** Event apps often optimize broadcast and discovery; attendees need a tiny social layer for temporary groups, location-specific memories, clips, outfits, and post-event follow-up.
- **Smallest prototype:** A one-event web app: join by QR, create a temporary roll, vote on best clips/photos, save contacts, then auto-archive after 72 hours.
- **Why it could spread:** It attaches to high-density social moments and can travel through event QR codes and recap links.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** The chart position may reflect a platform campaign, not durable independent behavior.
- **Fit for Max:** High; social product, creator funnel, and event-world design fit.
- **Evidence label:** **Interesting pattern**

## 3. Resale Fit Ledger

- **Trend signal:** Resale and closet behavior remain mainstream mobile behavior.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** Apple U.S. top-free feed showed Depop at #6, Vinted at #8, Whatnot at #23, and SHEIN/Shop in the top 40; paid feed showed Stylebook at #16.
- **Gap:** Marketplaces help people buy/sell, but they do not remember why an item worked, failed, duplicated another item, or should be sold now.
- **Smallest prototype:** Local closet ledger with wear events, almost-wears, sell/hold/repair suggestions, and links out to marketplace searches.
- **Why it could spread:** People already have closet guilt and resale loops; a ledger turns vague regret into a concrete action.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Manual closet logging is brittle unless capture is extremely lightweight.
- **Fit for Max:** Very high; direct fashion/taste engine and local-first personal software lane.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**

## 4. Short Drama Structure Lab

- **Trend signal:** Vertical short-drama apps are still visible in free app charts.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** Apple U.S. top-free feed showed DramaBox at #10, ReelShort at #29, and DramaWave at #32.
- **Gap:** Creators can see the format but lack a respectful structure tool for hooks, reversals, props, secrets, and cliffhangers without copying scripts.
- **Smallest prototype:** Paste public descriptions/transcripts and extract a beat card: initial wound, threat, reversal, status shift, cliffhanger, next-episode promise.
- **Why it could spread:** Creator education and media strategy teams want reusable structures, not plagiarism.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Short-drama ranking may be driven by paid acquisition and platform economics.
- **Fit for Max:** Medium-high; useful for content funnels and narrative product research.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**

## 5. Local Agent Boundary Kit

- **Trend signal:** Local-first personal agents are multiplying in public repos.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** GitHub API found `cgfixit/CyClaw`, described as secure/offline-first local AI agent; `trhariharasudhan/Grandpa`, described as a local-first personal AI assistant with Ollama and Windows automation; and `srikesh2654/personal-ai-assistant`, described as a local-first assistant with persistent memory, RAG, tool-calling agents, and desktop/CLI/phone interfaces.
- **Gap:** Local agents emphasize capability and memory more than visible boundaries, revocation, receipts, and per-domain rooms.
- **Smallest prototype:** A reusable `AGENT_ROOM.md` template plus small web viewer for scope, files, tools, expiration, receipts, and eviction.
- **Why it could spread:** Builders adopting local agents need a simple trust artifact before building full security infrastructure.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Developers may treat this as documentation overhead unless the viewer is beautiful and automatic.
- **Fit for Max:** Very high; directly maps to Aven/Hermes, local-first agents, and agent UX.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**

## 7. Smart Glasses Room Notice

- **Trend signal:** Smart-glasses recording anxiety is becoming consumer-app material.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** Apple U.S. top-paid feed showed `Antizuck Smart Glasses Scanner` at #5; GitHub API found `getnopeek/nopeek-android`, described as a real-time Bluetooth scanner detecting nearby smart glasses and VR headsets.

- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Device detection can be unreliable and social enforcement may be awkward.

## 9. Daily Note With Agent Receipts

- **Trend signal:** Daily-note and lightweight productivity tools still sell, while agent work needs audit trails.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** Apple paid feed showed `Parchment: Agenda & Daily Note` at #18 and `Things 3` at #40; GitHub local-agent search showed persistent-memory personal assistants.
- **Gap:** Daily notes capture human intentions, but not what agents did for those intentions across files, tools, and approvals.
- **Smallest prototype:** A local daily Markdown page that automatically embeds agent receipts: task, scope, files touched, decisions, unresolved risks, and rollback links.
- **Why it could spread:** It gives agent users one calm place to review what changed today.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** If receipts are noisy, users will ignore them.
- **Fit for Max:** Very high; Aven operating loop, local-first workflows, agent observability.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**
