# Rejected — Generic Virtual Try-On Clone

## Why it initially seemed promising
AI virtual try-on is visibly active across research and shopping platforms. It touches fashion, visual creation, ecommerce, social shopping, and personal taste — all Max-aligned territory.

## What weakened it
- Google and other large platforms already have direct catalog, product, and model advantages.
- arXiv activity suggests technical competition around image quality and controllability is dense.
- A generic VTON app does not solve the private longitudinal question: why did this garment fail after the render?
- Image-first try-on can increase desire without improving long-term wardrobe outcomes.

## Failed assumption
The failed assumption is that better fashion AI should start with better visual synthesis. The sharper primitive is fit/outcome memory.

## May become viable later
A VTON layer becomes more interesting if it plugs into a Fit Ledger and can test hypotheses against prior fit friction: “avoid this shoulder shape,” “try the larger size only in structured fabric,” “render with real layering context.”

## Useful remnants
- VTON sources remain useful for comparison and visual evidence capture.
- Try-on outputs can become one evidence field inside a Fit Ledger Card.
- Controllability research may help generate variant previews once the ledger knows which properties matter.

## Reconsider if
A model or platform exposes reliable garment/body/fit attributes that can be exported into user-owned local ledgers with negative outcomes and alteration history.
