# Source Notes — Taste Compilers

## Session signal

Taste evidence is scattered across fashion recommendations, creator shops, visual boards, self-hosted media, and AI creative workflows. The missing layer may be a compiler that turns artifacts into user-owned constraints before any recommender or generator acts.

## Interpretation

The source base points to convergence rather than a single breakthrough:

1. Fashion recommendation is technically active and increasingly multimodal/generative.
2. Creator commerce and visual discovery already contain strong taste evidence.
3. Local media custody and creative workflow graphs provide possible storage/execution substrates.
4. Negative feedback is becoming more explicit in styling research.

The sharper Vibe is a compiler: turn taste evidence into editable constraints before using it for shopping, generating, or ranking.

## Uncertainty

- Product pages prove source movement, not durable demand for explicit taste constraints.
- arXiv papers prove technical interest in fashion recommendation, not consumer desire for a visible constraint artifact.
- The capture interface may determine everything; if constraints require too much manual maintenance, the primitive dies.
