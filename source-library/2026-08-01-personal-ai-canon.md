# Source Notes — 2026-08-01 — Personal AI Needs a Behavioral Canon

## Primary signal

Local-first and personal-agent projects are converging around memory, tool access, skill learning, permission gates, digital twins, and personal operating systems. The interesting absence is a shared primitive for durable behavioral doctrine: what the agent should treat as standing operating culture rather than ordinary memory.

## Interpretation

**Supported:** There is active maker movement around local/personal agents with memory and permissions.

**Inferred:** Personal agents need a durable doctrine layer because memories, permissions, and transcripts alone do not encode how a specific person wants autonomous work to happen.

**Unknown:** Whether users will maintain canon directly, or whether canon must be mostly inferred from corrections and approvals.
