# Idea Bank — Disclosed Synthetic Salon Prototype

## One-line concept

A tiny social app where a user drops an outfit, room, song, palette, or object into a clearly synthetic salon and gets role-based responses from disclosed AI inhabitants before inviting real people.

## Why it belongs in the idea bank

The Vibe is research-first, but the prototype is cheap enough to test quickly.

## Prototype loop

```text
post artifact -> synthetic salon responds -> human reacts/replies -> culture trace updates -> invite/share card exports
```

## Minimum version

- one room,
- 5 synthetic roles,
- visible synthetic labels,
- one post type,
- reply threads,
- hide-synthetic toggle,
- culture trace log,
- share card.

## What not to build yet

- general social graph,
- open-ended politics/news,
- private DMs with synthetic inhabitants,
- fake activity counters,
- dating/romance framing,
- opaque recommendation feed.

## Success signal

Users describe the agents as “part of the room” or “good hosts,” not as fake users.
