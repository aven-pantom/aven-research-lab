# Trend Sparks

Trend Sparks are the recurring consumer-app opportunity lane inside Vibe Research.

They are not generic startup ideas. Each Spark starts from current evidence: visible social behavior, app-store/product movement, creator workflows, search/social trends, niche communities, or recently popular products. The goal is to notice app opportunities that are becoming culturally legible before they become obvious.

## Cadence

Every autonomous Vibe Research run should produce exactly **10 Trend Sparks** unless the run is explicitly scoped away from consumer/app opportunity work.

Create one file per run:

```text
idea-bank/trend-sparks/YYYY-MM-DD.md
```

## What counts

A good Trend Spark is:

- based on current trend evidence, not a generic brainstorm
- concrete enough to prototype
- culturally timed: explains why now
- aware of existing products that are close
- distinct from obvious AI-wrapper slop
- aligned with Max's worlds when possible: social products, fashion, creative tools, agents, local-first software, drones/aviation interfaces, creator funnels, and weird consumer utilities

## Required sections per run

Each file must include:

- Date
- Source scan
- Selection logic
- 10 numbered Trend Sparks
- Anti-slop filter
- Strongest three to watch
- Sources

## Required fields per Spark

For each of the 10 Trend Sparks include:

1. **Name** — memorable working title.
2. **Trend signal** — what is happening now.
3. **Existing proof** — apps/products/behaviors already visible.
4. **Gap** — what the current products miss.
5. **Smallest prototype** — one-week testable version.
6. **Why it could spread** — loop, share object, or social behavior.
7. **Risk / why it may be fake** — avoid hype-only reasoning.
8. **Fit for Max** — why this belongs in the lab.
9. **Evidence label** — Verified, Supported, Inferred, Speculative, Unknown, or Contradicted.

## Naming rule

Use **Trend Sparks** as the lane name. Do not label this output as just “Ideas”; the point is signal-derived app opportunity, not brainstorming.

## Quality bar

Reject Sparks that are only:

- “AI for X” without a new behavior loop
- copies of already dominant products
- monetization tactics without a real user pull
- trend-chasing without an original primitive
- app-store categories with no timing argument
- generic productivity/chatbot/wrapper suggestions

The best Sparks should feel like they could become Vibes, prototypes, or companies after deeper validation.
