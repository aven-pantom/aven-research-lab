# Rejected Path — Generic Mobile-Agent Leaderboard

Date: 2026-08-08

## Why it seemed promising

Mobile GUI-agent research is active, recent, and benchmark-heavy. A leaderboard or tracker could collect AndroidDaily, AndroidWorld, MobileGym, MobileWorld, GUIOdyssey, AppDeltaWorld, and related papers.

## What weakened it

A leaderboard would summarize the obvious frontier: task success. The stronger Vibe is not that phone agents will get better. The stronger Vibe is that task success creates a new trust failure on the most private device.

## Assumption that failed

The assumption that better evaluation equals the most useful research artifact. For Max, the higher-leverage primitive is a consumer/product-facing receipt that turns invisible permission decisions into a reviewable object.

## Useful remnants

Benchmark papers remain useful source material for the receipt schema, especially transition verification, process-aware evaluation, and closed-source app diagnostics.

## Future reconsideration signal

Reconsider a benchmark tracker only if it scores agents on permission minimality, boundary crossing, reversibility, redaction quality, and cleanup behavior — not task success alone.
