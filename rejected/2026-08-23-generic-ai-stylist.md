# Rejected Path — Generic AI Stylist

## Why it initially seemed promising

Fashion recommendation papers, creator commerce, visual boards, and virtual try-on all point toward demand for better styling help.

## What weakened it

A generic AI stylist collapses the signal into an obvious crowded product: upload closet, chat with stylist, generate outfits, shop links. That does not explain why current systems feel generic or what new primitive becomes possible.

## Failed assumption

The weak assumption was that the next step is better outfit generation. The stronger problem is upstream: personal taste evidence is not compiled into portable constraints.

## Useful remnants

- Fashion recommendation and negative-feedback styling research remain useful evidence.
- Creator-commerce and visual-board behavior are still strong taste surfaces.
- Virtual try-on can become downstream evidence once a constraint layer exists.

## Future signal that should trigger reconsideration

Reconsider a direct stylist product only if a small Taste Constraint Card fixture demonstrably improves outfit/recommendation quality over prompt-only or likes-only onboarding.
