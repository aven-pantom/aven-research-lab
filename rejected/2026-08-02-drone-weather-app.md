# Rejected — Generic Drone Weather App

Date: 2026-08-02

## Why it initially seemed promising

The source scan pointed toward drones, air taxis, HRRR, urban weather, and UTM. A simple interpretation would be to propose a drone-specific weather app.

## What weakened it

- **Verified:** NOAA HRRR public research page warns against safety-of-life decision use.
- **Inferred:** Consumer weather-app framing invites overconfidence in a safety-adjacent domain.
- **Inferred:** Drone operators already have weather sources; the missing primitive is likely route-local evidence, confidence, and operational explanation.

## Failed assumption

The failed assumption was that higher-resolution forecasts plus a drone UI would be enough.

## Useful remnants

- Forecast and sensor data still matter.
- A non-operational planning/simulation viewer may be useful.
- The route-card format may eventually sit inside a real weather service or UTM provider.

## Future signal that should trigger reconsideration

Reconsider if FAA/industry publishes clearer low-altitude weather service interfaces, or if a dense city-scale weather observation network becomes accessible with aviation-grade validation.

## Evidence label

**Supported** as a rejection of the generic app shape; **Unknown** for the eventual commercial weather-service opportunity.
