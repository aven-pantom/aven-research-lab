## Selection logic

Priority went to behavior loops Max can plausibly shape: creative tools, agent control, fashion/taste, local-first personal software, social rehearsal, creator funnels, aviation interfaces, and weird utilities. I favored signals with visible current proof from Apple RSS, GitHub API, or arXiv rather than broad trend guesses.

## Anti-slop filter

Rejected: generic AI chatbots, “AI wrapper for X,” thin monetization tools, clones of CapCut/TikTok/Depop without a new behavior loop, and ideas where the only proof was model hype. Each Spark below has a concrete smallest prototype and a reason it could spread beyond utility.

## 1. Reaction Card Video Editor

- **Trend signal:** arXiv `WorldExam` argues video/world-model evaluation must test world reactivity, while GitHub shows agent-drivable editors with JSON timelines and MCP.
- **Existing proof:** `FableCut`, `OpenChatCut`, and smaller repos expose timelines to agents; WorldExam separates visual quality from control, spatial consistency, and reactivity.
- **Gap:** AI video editors can record prompts and timelines, but they rarely show whether an edit produced the expected downstream consequence.
- **Smallest prototype:** Static 15-second timeline viewer with three Reaction Cards: pre-state, stimulus/edit, expected consequence, observed consequence, residual, confidence.
- **Why it could spread:** Creators can share “why this AI clip failed” cards and reusable scenario tests, not just prompts.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Creators may not want benchmark-like structure; the card must feel like creative control, not paperwork.
- **Fit for Max:** Very strong fit with Pantom creator tooling, provenance, agent receipts, and tasteful interface primitives.
- **Evidence label:** **Verified** for source signals; **Inferred** for product wedge.

## 2. Agent Timeline Repair Bot

- **Trend signal:** GitHub Search surfaced local-first/conversational video editors with MCP, REST, Agent Skills, Remotion, FFmpeg, and command-log timeline editing.
- **Existing proof:** `OpenChatCut` and `FableCut` show current developer demand for agent-editable timelines.
- **Gap:** Agent video editing still needs a repair loop: detect broken captions, jump cuts, missing assets, audio drift, bad crop, or failed command; propose a patch instead of regenerating blindly.
- **Smallest prototype:** Upload/export a JSON timeline, run a local checker, generate three patch suggestions with before/after diff cards.
- **Why it could spread:** “My agent fixed my timeline without touching the final cut” is a visible creator workflow win.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Timeline formats are fragmented; professional editors may already have enough repair tools.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with agent flight recorders, creative tooling, and local-first execution.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**.

## 3. Local Personal AI Dock

- **Trend signal:** GitHub Search on 2026-08-04 surfaced multiple local-first personal AI agent repos updated the same day, including desktop, Android, self-hosted, memory, scheduled work, skills, and permission-gated patterns.
- **Existing proof:** Repos such as `PersonalClaw`, `jenny-android-ai-agent`, `wolffish-app`, and `Fortress-Genie` indicate continuing developer movement around personal AI systems.
- **Gap:** Personal AI tools expose too much chat and too little operating-state: what it knows, what it is allowed to do, what is running, what needs approval, what expires.
- **Smallest prototype:** A dock/menubar/PWA status surface with memory domains, running tasks, permissions, hold/approve, and last receipt.
- **Why it could spread:** “My AI has a cockpit” is screenshot-legible and helps users trust background work.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Personal AI may remain a developer niche until reliability improves.
- **Fit for Max:** Very strong fit with Aven/Hermes, local-first agents, peripheral vision, and behavioral canon.
- **Evidence label:** **Verified** for repos; **Inferred** for consumer packaging.

## 4. Back-to-School Action Condenser

- **Trend signal:** Apple RSS showed ParentSquare at #5 in U.S. top-free apps on 2026-08-04.
- **Existing proof:** Parent-school communication has top-chart visibility during the current seasonal window.
- **Gap:** Parent communication apps deliver messages, but families still need a private action layer: who needs what, by when, with which form, fee, clothing, ride, or permission.
- **Smallest prototype:** Forward/import school messages into a local task-card condenser that extracts deadline, child, required action, proof, ambiguity, and calendar hold.
- **Why it could spread:** Back-to-school chaos is social; parents share “it reduced 38 messages to 5 actions.”
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Integrations are messy; privacy and school-platform lock-in can block distribution.
- **Fit for Max:** Medium: weird consumer/local-first agent utility, less core but behaviorally sharp.
- **Evidence label:** **Verified** for chart signal; **Inferred** for product gap.

## 5. Resale Closet Timing Radar

- **Trend signal:** Apple RSS showed Vinted and Depop both in U.S. top-free apps on 2026-08-04, with Whatnot also chart-visible as live/social commerce proof.
- **Existing proof:** Resale, social commerce, and closet liquidity remain mainstream consumer behaviors.
- **Gap:** Sellers still do manual listing labor; nobody turns almost-worn, skipped, or stale closet items into timing-aware resale prompts.
- **Smallest prototype:** 30-item closet board with wear/skip events, resale timing score, listing draft, and “why now” explanation for Vinted/Depop.
- **Why it could spread:** “Your closet has money trapped in almost-wears” is personal, visual, and shareable.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Listing fatigue may overpower insights; resale platforms may own listing workflows.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with taste engines, fashion, negative taste, and consumer app loops.
- **Evidence label:** **Verified** for chart signal; **Inferred** for opportunity.

## 6. Taste-Boundary Stylist

- **Trend signal:** GitHub Search surfaced AI wardrobe/fashion assistant projects updated 2026-08-04, while Apple RSS showed Vinted and Depop chart-visible.
- **Existing proof:** Developer and consumer signals both point toward wardrobe digitization, styling, resale, and outfit recommendations.
- **Gap:** Most AI stylists optimize for suggestions and virtual try-on; they underuse negative taste: skips, almost-wears, returns, “too polished,” “wrong proportion,” “not me.”
- **Smallest prototype:** Weekly outfit session that logs five rejects, three almost-wears, and two accepted looks, then updates a local Taste Boundary Card.
- **Why it could spread:** People like explaining why something is not them; rejection cards are more socially specific than generic recommendations.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Users may not maintain the log unless the first session feels immediately perceptive.
- **Fit for Max:** Very strong fit with fashion/taste worlds and local-first personal data.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**.

## 7. Short-Drama Beat Auditor

- **Trend signal:** Apple RSS showed DramaBox and CapCut both chart-visible on 2026-08-04.
- **Existing proof:** Short-drama consumption and mobile video editing have mainstream app-store presence.
- **Gap:** Creators can edit quickly but lack a format-aware auditor for hook, reveal, subtitle timing, emotional turn, cliffhanger, and thumbnail promise.
- **Smallest prototype:** Paste a 60-second script or rough cut notes; receive a beat card with hook timing, reveal cadence, dead seconds, caption risk, and reshoot prompt.
- **Why it could spread:** Creators share beat cards and compete on retention structure.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Could collapse into generic creator advice unless tied to actual clips and revisions.
- **Fit for Max:** Medium-high: creator funnels and video systems, but needs taste discipline.
- **Evidence label:** **Verified** for app-chart signal; **Speculative** for spread loop.

## 8. AI Feed Rehearsal Room

- **Trend signal:** Apple RSS showed Threads, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram, and Meta AI chart-visible; previous lab work identified AI-only social rooms as sharper when framed as rehearsal, not fake crowds.
- **Existing proof:** Social posting remains central, and AI assistant apps are mainstream enough to sit beside social apps in the feed.
- **Gap:** Before posting, creators need a disclosed imaginary room that reacts like specific audiences: close friends, harsh taste people, confused public, future customer, brand-safe editor.
- **Smallest prototype:** Upload post/outfit/caption; choose a Room Contract; receive disagreement, likely misread, suggested edit, and exportable revision card.
- **Why it could spread:** People share the room’s harsh/funny critique and the before/after improvement.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Synthetic validation can become psychological junk unless disagreement and debrief are built in.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with social products, taste engines, and creator critique tools.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**.

## 9. AI App Receipt Shelf

- **Trend signal:** Apple RSS showed ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Meta AI all chart-visible on 2026-08-04.
- **Existing proof:** Consumers are using multiple AI apps, but switching between them leaves no coherent record of what was asked, approved, exported, or trusted.
- **Gap:** Users need a private receipt shelf across AI apps: decisions, prompts, files touched, sources, outputs, confidence, and follow-up obligations.
- **Smallest prototype:** Manual/local browser extension or clipboard-first PWA where users save AI interactions as structured receipt cards with source/output links.
- **Why it could spread:** “Receipts for what my AI told me” is legible as AI use becomes normal and risky.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Platform APIs are limited; manual saving may be too much friction.
- **Fit for Max:** Very strong fit with agent receipts, Orior dossiers, provenance, and personal software.
- **Evidence label:** **Verified** for AI app chart presence; **Inferred** for need.

## 10. Drone GCS Briefing Card

- **Trend signal:** GitHub Search surfaced current ground-control and drone-control app repositories, including browser-connected and mobile/Windows GCS patterns.
- **Existing proof:** Open drone work continues around ground control, MAVLink/DJI, Raspberry Pi/web frontends, and device-based control surfaces.
- **Gap:** GCS interfaces expose controls but not a calm mission-briefing card: route, weather assumption, firmware state, airspace note, module status, failsafe, and human-readable abort condition.
- **Smallest prototype:** Non-operational simulator-only briefing-card viewer for a fictional route and fictional N1 Scout module state.
- **Why it could spread:** Drone builders share mission briefs/build logs; a polished evidence card makes autonomy work more legible.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Hardware and airspace claims become safety-sensitive quickly; keep simulator-only until verified.
- **Fit for Max:** Strong fit with Notrex/N1 Scout and aviation interface design.
- **Evidence label:** **Verified** for repo signal; **Inferred** for product wedge.
