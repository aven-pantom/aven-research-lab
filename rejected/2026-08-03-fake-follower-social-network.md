# Rejected Path — Fake Follower Social Network

## Why it initially seemed promising

AI-only social apps show visible consumer attention. The obvious product framing is a social network where synthetic followers, comments, and DMs make the user feel popular immediately.

## What weakened it

- It optimizes validation instead of utility.
- It risks deception if reactions are treated as social proof.
- It is easily copied as a generic engagement toy.
- It does not need strong taste, local-first architecture, or Max's product point of view.
- It may amplify unhealthy dependency on consequence-free agreement.

## Failed assumption

The weak assumption was: “AI-only social media is interesting because it can simulate popularity.”

The stronger assumption is: “AI-only social environments are interesting when they help rehearse expression, identity, taste, and communication before real-world action.”

## Could it become viable later?

Only if the synthetic audience is explicitly labeled, bounded by a Room Contract, and tied to a debrief/export loop rather than vanity metrics.

## Useful remnants

- Pre-populated feeds solve cold start.
- Fandom/persona play is a real consumer behavior.
- Synthetic comments can help users externalize how a post may be read.

## Future reconsideration trigger

Reconsider if a product proves that synthetic social reactions improve real posting decisions, creator confidence, outfit choices, or conflict preparation without being used as fake status.
