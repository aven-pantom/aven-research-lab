# Rejected Path — Generic AI Video Editor

## Why it initially seemed promising

AI video, short-form creator tools, and prompt-to-motion graphics are visibly active. A simple “AI video editor for creators” sounds aligned with current demand.

## What weakened it

- **Verified:** App-store and HN signals show this lane is already crowded by large incumbents, indie editors, video generators, and compiler-like experiments.
- **Inferred:** A generic editor would compete on generation quality, templates, and distribution rather than a distinct primitive.

## Failed assumption

The missing product is not another editor surface. The missing primitive is replayable creative intent across generation, edits, review, provenance, and reuse.

## Useful remnants

- AI-native video editing is still a strong source field.
- Prompt-to-motion and video-as-code signals make the intent timeline direction sharper.
- CapCut/short-drama visibility keeps the creator-consumer loop relevant.

## Future reconsideration trigger

Reconsider a full editor only if an Intent Timeline Card prototype proves creators need an opinionated interface rather than a portable project-memory layer.
