## Selection logic

Selected opportunities that show a repeated behavior, an existing proof point, a missing primitive, and a smallest version Max could plausibly shape with taste. Priority went to social products, fashion/taste engines, creative tools, agent interfaces, local-first personal software, aviation/drone interfaces, and weird consumer utilities.

## Anti-slop filter

Rejected patterns:

- Generic AI companions with no distinct behavior loop.
- AI-only social products framed as fake popularity rather than disclosed rehearsal or play.
- CapCut/Videoleap clones.
- Outfit generators that ignore rejects, almost-wears, and context.
- Drone utilities that imply operational authority or tactical tracking.
- Local notes apps that are only chat over files without memory boundaries.
- Habit trackers that only add streaks and leaderboards.

## 1. Imaginary Room Rehearsal

- **Name:** Imaginary Room Rehearsal
- **Trend signal:** AI-only social apps are appearing as a visible consumer category.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** iTunes Search showed `status - sims but social media` with 188,980 ratings, `Aspect: AI-Only Social Media` with 8,769, `Fomo - AI Social Network` with 2,409, and `Charm: AI Only Social Media` with 75. Store copy repeatedly says the user is the only human and AI accounts comment, DM, post, follow, or roleplay.
- **Gap:** Current framing leans toward synthetic attention; the missing utility is honest rehearsal before publishing, dressing, launching, or messaging.
- **Smallest prototype:** One web card: paste a post/outfit/launch line, choose three disclosed rooms, receive predicted misreadings, useful edits, disagreement, and a publish/revise decision.
- **Why it could spread:** People already draft for imagined audiences; making the imagined audience visible and shareable creates a repeatable ritual.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Users may mainly want validation, not rehearsal; synthetic feedback can create false confidence.
- **Fit for Max:** Very high; social product cold starts, taste engines, creative direction, local agent rooms.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**

## 2. Taste Room for Outfits

- **Name:** Taste Room for Outfits
- **Trend signal:** Outfit planning remains a high-traction app category.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** iTunes Search showed `Combyne` with 87,057 ratings, `Alta Daily` with 11,664, `Whering` with 10,992, and `Fits` with 4,873.
- **Gap:** Apps help pick outfits, but rarely simulate how an outfit reads across contexts: buyer, stylist, close friend, work, date, resale, weather.
- **Smallest prototype:** Text/photo outfit card with five taste-room reactions and one action: wear, alter, resell, save, reject.
- **Why it could spread:** “How does this read?” is a daily social question and screenshot-native behavior.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** If roles are generic, the output is just outfit-generator slop.
- **Fit for Max:** Very high; directly connects fashion, negative taste events, and social simulation.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**

## 3. Wrong-But-Close Reference Board

- **Name:** Wrong-But-Close Reference Board
- **Trend signal:** Moodboard tools have durable demand and creators need sharper language for taste boundaries.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** iTunes Search showed `Moodboard` with 1,709 ratings, `Morpholio Board` with 3,749, and `Milanote` with 3,557.
- **Gap:** Boards capture inspiration but not near-misses: too sterile, too loud, too brand-like, too literal, too generic.
- **Smallest prototype:** Upload/drop 12 references; mark three as wrong-but-close; export a Taste Boundary Card for an agent/designer.
- **Why it could spread:** Negative examples are useful, visual, and easy to share in creative workflows.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Extraction may be vague unless the product forces concrete boundary language.
- **Fit for Max:** Very high; Orior/Pantom creative direction and design-taste memory.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**

## 4. Screen Work to Agent Lesson

- **Name:** Screen Work to Agent Lesson
- **Trend signal:** Screen recording is mainstream, while agent products are trying to learn from user workflows.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** iTunes Search showed `Record it!` with 450,722 ratings and `Go Record` with 110,577. HN surfaced Screenpipe: “Record how you work and turn that into agents.”
- **Gap:** Recordings are heavy artifacts; agents need scoped lessons with timestamps, exceptions, approvals, and boundaries.
- **Smallest prototype:** Record a 60-second workflow and generate an editable Agent Lesson Card: trigger, steps, forbidden actions, uncertainty, replay bounds.
- **Why it could spread:** People already record workflows for humans; converting them into agent memory is a natural next step.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Hidden context and credentials can make extracted procedures unsafe.
- **Fit for Max:** Very high; Hermes skills, local-first agents, procedure cards.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**

## 5. Browser Agent Trust Badge

- **Name:** Browser Agent Trust Badge
- **Trend signal:** Browser automation for AI agents is moving through maker channels.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** HN/Algolia surfaced `Agent-Browser – Browser Automation for AI` on 2026-08-02, `OpenTrust – Browser trust signals for the AI era` on 2026-07-23, and `chrome-agent` on 2026-07-20.
- **Gap:** Browser-agent users cannot see whether a site/action is safe, risky, reversible, prohibited, stale, or receipt-required.
- **Smallest prototype:** Browser extension mock: current page shows allowed machine actions, risks, last receipt, and hold/approve button.
- **Why it could spread:** The first bad browser-agent incidents will make legibility feel necessary.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Sites may not publish enough machine-action metadata; vendors may own the badge.
- **Fit for Max:** High; connects Street Signs, Flight Recorders, and agent permission UX.
- **Evidence label:** **Interesting pattern**

## 6. Creator Clip Debrief Card

- **Name:** Creator Clip Debrief Card
- **Trend signal:** AI video editing and creator clipping tools are active and crowded.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** iTunes Search showed `CapCut` with 1,116,991 ratings, `Videoleap` with 145,002, `VideoGPT` with 50,483, and `Vids AI` with 44,887. HN surfaced `Reclip – AI Video Clipper, Voiceover and Creator Tools` on 2026-07-31.
- **Gap:** Most tools produce clips, but not reusable debriefs: why this hook works, where attention drops, which edit rule to keep next time.
- **Smallest prototype:** Upload/paste one short; generate a Clip Debrief Card with hook, pacing, repeated structure, reusable rule, and next variant.
- **Why it could spread:** Creators want repeatable formats, not only one-off outputs.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Without real retention data, debriefs may be plausible guesses.
- **Fit for Max:** High; creator funnel and intent timeline adjacency.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**

## 7. Flight Pickup Live Card

- **Name:** Flight Pickup Live Card
- **Trend signal:** Flight tracking remains a massive utility ritual.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** iTunes Search showed `Flightradar24` with 647,811 ratings, `FlightAware` with 379,087, `Flight Tracker +` with 289,219, and `Flighty` with 144,499.
- **Gap:** Trackers are aircraft-centric; pickup coordination needs a human-centric card: inbound status, gate/bag uncertainty, pickup window, who needs to move next.
- **Smallest prototype:** Forward itinerary or enter flight; generate a shareable pickup card with next action and uncertainty labels.
- **Why it could spread:** Airport pickup is social coordination under anxiety; a calm card is shareable by default.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Data access/reliability can be poor; incumbents already own flight state.
- **Fit for Max:** High; aviation UX and weird consumer utility.
- **Evidence label:** **Supported**

## 8. Drone Neighbor Manners Card

- **Name:** Drone Neighbor Manners Card
- **Trend signal:** Consumer drone apps and airspace-safety tools exist, but bystanders remain confused.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** iTunes Search showed `Drone Buddy` with 8,422 ratings, `DJI Fly` with 4,527, and FAA `B4UFLY Drone Airspace Safety` with 499.
- **Gap:** Most apps serve pilots; neighbors need non-operational education: what can be inferred, what cannot, when to ignore/report, and what not to do.
- **Smallest prototype:** Static educational card generator for local drone questions with safety/legal caveats and no tactical tracking.
- **Why it could spread:** Civil autonomy needs public legibility, not only pilot compliance.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Must avoid doxxing, tracking, or unsafe claims.
- **Fit for Max:** High; drone/aviation interfaces and autonomy manners.
- **Evidence label:** **Interesting pattern**

## 9. Local Notes Boundary Box

- **Name:** Local Notes Boundary Box
- **Trend signal:** AI note-taking is crowded, with a small local-first counter-signal.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** iTunes Search showed `Turbo AI - Notetaker` with 33,308 ratings, `Voicenotes AI Notes & Meetings` with 6,593, and smaller listings such as `Note Taker AI - 100% local` and `Local AI Notes`.
- **Gap:** Notes apps promise recall, but users need memory boundaries: what the agent may remember, forget, summarize, cite, or never use.
- **Smallest prototype:** Local folder note app with per-note memory tags: private, citeable, temporary, never-train, expires, ask-before-use.
- **Why it could spread:** Personal AI trust will depend on visible memory policy, not only better search.

## 10. Proof Ring for Friends

- **Name:** Proof Ring for Friends
- **Trend signal:** Habit apps and friend accountability remain persistent categories.
- **Existing proof:** **Verified:** iTunes Search showed `Habit Tracker` with 144,824 ratings, `HabitShare` with 653, and `HabitLink` with 314.
- **Gap:** Streaks are generic; small groups need proof artifacts, friction notes, and weekly identity recaps.
- **Smallest prototype:** Seven-day private ring with one daily proof card, one friction note, one witness reaction, and a weekly recap.
- **Why it could spread:** Small groups create enough pressure without needing a public network.
- **Risk / why it may be fake:** Habit products decay quickly if the identity loop is weak.
- **Fit for Max:** Medium-high; social product loop and creator/community funnel adjacency.
- **Evidence label:** **Interesting pattern**
